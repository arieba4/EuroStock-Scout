What the score means
Beta fit 30% — historical beta against the EXSA European-market ETF proxy.
Trend 25% — price above SMA20/SMA50 and positive moving-average structure.
RSI 20% — rewards constructive momentum, but penalizes overbought readings.
Momentum 15% — 20-session rate of change.
Risk 10% — ATR as a percentage of price; excessive volatility scores lower.
Signal rules
BUY ZONE: score ≥ 68, beta inside tolerance, RSI 42–68, and trend not broken.
WAIT: acceptable stock but entry is stretched, momentum is weak, or score is 55–67.
AVOID: beta outside tolerance by too much, score <55, or trend/risk profile is poor.

Entry is normally between current price and roughly 0.35 ATR below it. Target is the selected 3–4% above modeled entry. Stop is ATR-based and is deliberately independent of the profit target.
Decision-support tool only, not individualized investment advice. A 3–4% target is an objective, not an expected or guaranteed return. Market gaps can pass through a stop. Check spreads, commissions, taxes, corporate events and material news before trading. Beta and technical indicators are backward-looking.
