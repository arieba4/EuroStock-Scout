Ratings and dividends
Scout Grade is the app's A+–F technical grade. It is not a Morningstar grade. Morningstar Stock Rating remains a link to official Morningstar research because Morningstar's star rating is proprietary data and is not included in Marketstack's free EOD feed.

Dividend yield: v2.2 tries Marketstack's dividend endpoint only for the highest-ranked names. If your Marketstack plan does not include dividend history, the field displays “N/A” without breaking the stock scan.
Decision-support tool only, not individualized investment advice. End-of-day data can be delayed relative to the current trading session. Dividend yield is trailing and may change or be cut. A 3–4% target is not guaranteed. Beta and technical indicators are backward-looking.
