Understanding beta diagnostics
Stock volatility and benchmark volatility are annualized from the same aligned daily returns used for beta. Correlation ranges from −1 to +1. The volatility ratio is stock volatility divided by benchmark volatility. For the same observations, beta is approximately correlation × volatility ratio. This makes unusually high or negative beta values easier to audit.
Ratings and dividends
Scout Grade is the app's A+–F grade from beta fit, trend, RSI, 20-day momentum and ATR risk. Morningstar remains a research link because its star rating is proprietary. Dividend yield is calculated from trailing dividend records when Marketstack returns them.
